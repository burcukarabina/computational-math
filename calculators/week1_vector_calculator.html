🚁 Mission 1: Tracking a Drone's Position

A drone follows a straight flight path passing through \(P=(1,1,2)\) with direction vector

\[ \vec d= \begin{bmatrix} 2\\1\\2 \end{bmatrix}. \]

Your Mission: Determine the drone's position when \(t=2\).

Recall that the vector equation of a line is

\[ \vec x=\vec p+t\vec d. \]

Before using the calculator: Which vector operations are required to determine the drone's position?

Use the calculator to perform the calculations. Then interpret the coordinates of the resulting point.
